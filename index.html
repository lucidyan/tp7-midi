Track Mixer CC 7 / CC 120

ℹ️ 6 stereo tracks controllable via MIDI. USB only outputs tracks 1-3, but TP-7 can mix all 6 internally.
⚠️ Resets on track change! Mixer levels and mutes reset when track is changed via cue or loop.

Rocker CC 18 Stopped

ℹ️ 1 of 3 speed controls. Emulates physical rocker. Works with AND without Play. NOT the SPD on screen!
⚠️ Playback Mode (After Play or Cue trigger): CAN'T STOP! Value 64 no longer stops - center shifts! 0-60 = reverse, 61-127 = forward. Slowest at 60/61. To stop: CC18=60 + Pitch Bend=+708.
When ON, Stop also sends Pitch Bend +708 to force stop during playback/cue mode
