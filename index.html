Track Mixer CC 7 / CC 120

ℹ️ 6 stereo tracks controllable via MIDI. USB only outputs tracks 1-3, but TP-7 can mix all 6 internally.
⚠️ Resets on track change! Mixer levels and mutes reset when track is changed via cue or loop.

DJ Jump Grid Beat / bar jumps

Waiting for the current track…

Beat 1 starts at 0:00. Use the BPM of the original file, even when playback speed is changed. Beats are quarter notes (4 beats per bar for 4/4). The grid assumes a constant tempo; it does not detect drums.

Generated time jumps, saved only in this browser; existing TP-7 cues stay intact. A tap stops playback, seeks and resumes. Positions are rounded once to the nearest millisecond. Bluetooth and device response add latency: jumps are not quantized to a live beat or synchronized with another deck.
