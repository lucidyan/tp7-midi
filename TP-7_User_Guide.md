## Connectivity

### Two-Way Jacks / Main Out

The TP-7 main output is located on the bottom front side of the unit. It is used for the main stereo audio output signal, and holds an included, removable 6.35 mm jack to 3.5 mm mini jack adapter.

The three 3.5 mm mini jack sockets on the top side of TP-7 are two-way jacks, which can be configured as either stereo inputs or outputs, or for headset use. You can connect equipment such as headphones, studio monitors, microphones, or any other audio equipment, such as TX-6 and OP-1 field.

### USB-C

The USB type C port can be used as a multi channel audio interface, as well as for MIDI control, firmware updates and charging.

### Layout

**Top Side:**
- 3.5 mm two-way jacks (3x)
- Internal mic (front side)

**Bottom Side:**
- USB-C
- Main out

**Right Side:**
- Memo button (for making voice memo recordings)
- User buttons (up and down) - used for setting input gain and displaying file information

**Left Side:**
- Rocker - lets you fast forward and rewind the tape
- Internal speaker - plays your audio automatically when nothing else is connected
- Mode button - lets you access and toggle mode and menus in different contexts

---

## Input and Output Examples

When using the two-way jacks as inputs, they can be individually configured as stereo, mono, or balanced mono, in any combination. When using them as headset inputs you can connect three separate headsets, such as the M-1 personal monitor headsets.

As outputs, you can connect to external speakers, headphones or anything with a line-in connector.

The main output gives you a stereo mix of any audio recorded, or played, through the unit. Use the included jack to mini jack adapter if needed.

The USB-C can be used as a class compliant multi channel audio interface. Depending on what it is connected to, you can get up to six stereo channel audio, in and out.

## Charging

The unit is charged through the USB-C port. Battery level is indicated on the screen when charging, in MTP mode and through the system menu.

**Battery Indicators:**
- If the battery is low, the top LED turns red
- Flashes red on critically low battery level
- When charging and fully charged, the top LED turns white

To keep the battery healthy, the unit should be charged at least every 6 months.

## Power

To power on TP-7, turn the power knob clockwise, until you feel and hear a click. The display will briefly show the TP-7 power up title screen before arriving at the playback screen.

Keep turning the knob to adjust the master volume. Always make sure to be careful with your ears.

To power off TP-7, turn the knob counterclockwise, past the click.

## Guide Conventions

Sometimes you will need to press buttons in sequence, sometimes in combination:

- **Press** - tap the button one time
- **Hold** - press and keep it pressed down
- **Sequence** - first press one button and then the other
- **Simultaneous** - press both buttons at the same time
- **Hold + Rotate** - press and hold the first button, and rotate the reel
- **Press + Rotate** - first press one button and then rotate the reel

Many of the knobs and buttons have different functionality depending on the context.

## Modes

Your TP-7 field tape recorder has three main playback modes. Tap the mode button on the left side to toggle between these modes:

### Memo
Used to access all your quick and simple voice memos, recorded through the memo function. Memo is where you capture and playback voice memo recordings. It's the fastest way to record. Memo uses the internal microphone to capture audio.

### Recordings
For all other recordings made on the device. The recording mode is where you get access to the multitrack recording functionality.

### Library
A high resolution audio playback mode, where external content is loaded to the device for playback. Library mode is your high resolution audio file music player. Transfer your files from a computer and play them back on TP-7.

## Recording

Press record to ready a new recording. The recording light will start to flash.

Press play to start recording. The display shows a counter with recorded time. Press stop to stop recording and save the file.

### Recording Sources

You can record from three different sources:
- Using the internal mic
- Using the analog inputs
- Digitally using USB-C

The internal microphone will be used by default, if nothing else is connected to TP-7.

You'll find the internal mic discreetly placed to the left of the top LED.

If you've connected either an analog or digital input, those will be used along with the internal microphone.

### Recording Process

1. Press record to arm TP-7 for recording
2. Press play to start recording
3. Pause your recording by simply holding the tape reel; release it to keep recording
4. Press stop to stop your recording
5. Your latest recording will appear once you have pressed stop
6. Skip through your recordings with the plus/minus buttons
7. Press play to instantly listen to your recording
8. Scrub through your recording using the tape reel
9. Use the rocker to fast forward/rewind
10. Press stop to exit the playback

### Recording Configurations

#### Analog Recording
With all jacks configured as line inputs, you can record analog signal devices with tape in multitrack mode. Use three analog inputs in any variation or set your jacks to 'headset' from the system menu.

#### Digital Recording
The USB-C port can be used to record in various scenarios:
- 1 track: CM-15
- 3 tracks: computer
- 6 tracks: TX-6 (12 channel mode)

#### Analog/Digital Combined
When connected to TX-6 in 2-channel mode, you can combine it with any analog input. Set it to any of the three tracks you prefer. This setup is great when combining multiple analog sources with a digital source.

### Track Configuration

1. Enter rec armed mode
2. The number of tracks used will be auto detected based on what is connected to your TP-7
3. Press plus to enter track view
4. If multiple inputs are connected, press plus/minus to select your tracks
5. Press mode to toggle the input source alternatives
6. To add a track, press and hold plus, then confirm by pressing plus again
7. Press mode to toggle input source alternatives
8. Remove a track by holding minus and confirm by pressing minus again

**Pro-tip:** In armed rec mode, hold up to show current input overview.

### Input Alternatives

There are four different input alternatives:
- Nothing (silence)
- Internal mic
- Analog
- Digital

When selecting a track, adjust the input gain by pressing the up and down user buttons.

## Edit Recording

In TP-7, you can make edits to your recordings by using edit mode. You have the option to either replace or mix your existing tracks with the new edit.

**Important:** The original recording cannot be reverted back to its original state, so make sure you're ready to commit.

### Edit Mode Setup

1. Access the system settings to set the edit mode
2. Scroll to select either 'mix' or 'replace'
   - **Mix:** Will mix the recording together with your new edit
   - **Replace:** Will replace the recorded track with your new edit

### Using Edit Mode

1. After exiting the system menu, select the recording you want to edit
2. Press the record and stop buttons together to toggle the edit mode
3. The number of tracks will appear in the bottom left of the screen
4. Scrub the tape reel to set the desired in-point
5. Scrub back before the start of the file and start recording - the recording will then start at zero
6. Press play to start the edit
7. Press stop to stop the edit
8. Hold your finger on the reel to pause
9. To exit edit mode and return to normal recording mode, press the rec and stop button together again

### Edit Mode Features

- In edit mode you can both playback and record
- Hit record to toggle between recording and playback
- You can always cycle through the file tracks with plus/minus
- The tracks without a selected input will be left untouched
- The tracks with a selected input will be overwritten/mixed depending on the setting in the system menu
- The armed recording edit mode is indicated by the flashing recording light

### Edit Mode Tips

**Replace mode** is useful when you want to replace an existing recording with an entirely new one. For instance, if you recorded a guitar solo but you want to re-record it, you can use replace mode to erase the old take and record a new one in its place.

**Mix mode** is useful if you want to layer multiple recordings. If you want to add harmonies to a vocal track, use mix mode to overdub the new recording on top of the existing one. This allows you to create complex arrangements with multiple layers of instruments and vocals.

## Gain

For every scenario you are recording, from vocals to instruments, you will need to adjust the gain levels to achieve the best quality sound.

As you adjust the gain level, you will see the decibel number displayed on the screen. The LEDs on the bottom right of tape will indicate your levels, so you can see when you are in a safe zone or if you're at risk of clipping.

### Analog Gain
- While in rec armed mode (analog), set the gain by pressing up/down
- Minimum gain: 0 dB
- Maximum gain: +42 dB

### Fixed Gain (USB-C and Internal Microphone)
When selecting either the USB-C input or the internal mic, the gain settings will be fixed by default.

**Note:** If the CM-15 microphone is connected to TP-7 with USB, its gain can be adjusted from -12dB to +12dB.

## Playback

With playback you can listen back to any track you have recorded and stored on your device. Use the built-in speaker or connect an external speaker/headphones to listen.

Use the mode button to choose to listen from your collection of memos, recordings, or from the library.

### Playback Controls

1. In any mode, press play to start the playback
2. Hold the reel to pause the playback
3. Release it to continue the playback
4. Rotate the reel in any direction to scrub the recording
5. You can also use the rocker to fast forward and rewind
6. Press stop to stop playback
7. Press stop again to reset to the beginning of the file

### Playback Speed and Direction

1. To adjust the playback speed while playing a track, press the mode button
2. The speed adjuster will appear, set to 0 by default
3. Rotate right to speed it up (+200% playback speed maximum)
4. Rotate left to slow it down (-50% playback speed minimum)
5. Press play to start the playback and press play again to reverse the playback direction

### Playback Settings

You can set how playback works from the system settings:

**Resume:** The next track will load automatically when a track ends

**Stop:** The device will stop after the track has been played

**Repeat:** The repeat setting will start your playback again when the track ends

## Tape Reel Motor

The tape reel motor can be configured in three ways:

**Play and Rec:** The motor is activated and the tape reel will spin both in rec and play mode, as well as being able to use it for scrubbing.

**Play:** The motor is activated and the tape reel will spin but only in play mode, as well as being able to use it for scrubbing.

**Off:** The motor is off and will not spin or scrub.

When switched off the tape reel will not spin at all, nor can it be used to scrub your recordings. This comes in handy when carrying it in your pocket as a music player or at times when you want to be more discrete.

Access the motor settings from the system menu.

## Library

The library is where you can store and access external audio files from your computer, to use for playback, for overdubbing, creating your own unique tracks in TP-7, or for DJing, selecting tracks and using the tape reel to scratch.

TP-7 can also store other file types, and be used as an external storage device for your computer. These files can only be accessed from the computer and not from the menu.

### Using the Library

- 6x tape playback

### Outputs
- Internal speaker

- 6 separate channels on USB
- 3 separate channels to line out if multichannel player mode
